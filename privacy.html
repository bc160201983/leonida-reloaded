Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what the LeonidaReloaded desktop app does with data. The app is used only by the channel owner, on the owner's own computer.

Data from TikTok

When the owner connects TikTok, the app receives from TikTok: the account's open ID, display name and avatar (to show which account it will post to), the account's posting options (privacy levels and comment, Duet and Stitch settings), and access tokens that let it upload videos the owner chooses to send.

How it is used

This data is used only to show the connected account and to upload the owner's own videos to that account when the owner asks. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Where it is stored

Everything stays on the owner's computer. Access tokens are stored encrypted and are never sent anywhere except to TikTok. No data is sold or shared with third parties. This website collects no personal data and uses no cookies or trackers.

Deleting data

Disconnecting TikTok in the app deletes the stored tokens and asks TikTok to revoke them. Access can also be removed at any time in TikTok's settings (Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions).

Children

The app is not directed at children.
