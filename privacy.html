In short

The app is used only by the channel owner, on the owner's own computer. TikTok data stays on that computer, is encrypted, and is never sold or shared. This website collects nothing.

Data from TikTok

When the owner connects TikTok, the app receives:

  • the account's open ID, display name and avatar, to show which account it will post to;
  • the account's posting options (privacy levels, and comment, Duet and Stitch settings);
  • access tokens that let it upload the videos the owner chooses to send.

How it is used

This data is used only to show the connected account and to upload the owner's own videos to that account when the owner asks. It is not used for advertising, profiling or anything else.

Where it is stored

Everything stays on the owner's computer. Access tokens are stored encrypted and are only ever sent to TikTok. No data is sold or shared with third parties.

This website

This website collects no personal data and uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Deleting data

Disconnecting TikTok in the app deletes the stored tokens and asks TikTok to revoke them. Access can also be removed at any time in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy › Security › Manage app permissions.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Message the channel on YouTube.
